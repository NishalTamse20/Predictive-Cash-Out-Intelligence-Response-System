How to read these results

  • Risk scoreA 0–100 heuristic indicator from the transaction trail, not a fraud probability.
  • Candidate rankingAn ordering of possible locations to investigate, not evidence that a withdrawal occurred.
  • Estimated time windowA fixed illustrative interval after report time, not a trained or validated prediction.
  • Synthetic dataArtificial case records and outcomes created for this prototype, not observed incidents.
PaySim is simulated. OSM, if used, contributes mapped geography only and no historical cash-out activity. This prototype has not been validated against real complaints or withdrawals.
